About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2002 by Kemraj "Kem" Persaud, Kem's Bakery began as a small family operation in the bustling heart of Georgetown, Guyana. What started as a humble corner bakery selling traditional Guyanese breads and pastries has grown into one of the city's most beloved baking establishments.

Kem learned the art of baking from his grandmother, who ran a small bake shop in New Amsterdam. Her recipes, passed down through three generations, form the foundation of our menu today. From the iconic coconut bread to the rich black cake served at weddings across the country, each item carries a piece of our heritage.

Over the years, we've expanded our offerings while staying true to our roots. We source local ingredients whenever possible, supporting Guyanese farmers and producers. Our commitment to quality and authenticity has earned us a loyal customer base that spans generations.

Our Values

Tradition

We honor the time-tested baking methods and recipes that have defined Caribbean baking for generations. Every loaf and pastry is crafted with respect for our culinary heritage.

Quality

We never compromise on ingredients or craftsmanship. From premium flours to fresh local butter, every component is selected to ensure the highest standard of excellence.

Community

We believe in giving back to the Georgetown community that has supported us. Through donations, sponsorships, and employment opportunities, we strive to make a positive impact.
